About

Play SWF files in your browser, from a StuxAPIs service.

FlashView plays Flash content (SWF files) in any modern browser, with no plugin. Open a file from your device or a link, and it plays on the page. It's built on Ruffle, an open-source Flash Player emulator written in Rust, which FlashView hosts itself.

What it does

  • Plays an SWF from your computer (pick it, or drop it on the player). The file is read by your browser and never uploaded.
  • Plays an SWF from a link, and gives you a ?url= link to share it.
  • Hosts Ruffle for other sites: one script tag brings their old Flash embeds back. See Embed.

What plays

Ruffle supports most ActionScript 1 and 2 content (most Flash games and animations from before 2010), and a growing share of ActionScript 3. If something doesn't play, it's usually a Ruffle limitation; their compatibility page says what works.
