Opt-Out Preferences

The short version: there's nothing to opt out of.

No data sale or sharing

Laws like California's CCPA/CPRA give you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. FlashView collects no personal data for advertising or analytics, so there's none to sell or share.

What actually happens

Everything FlashView needs, Ruffle and the fonts included, comes from this same domain. Files you open stay on your device. Links you play are fetched by your browser straight from their own site.

Do Not Track

FlashView runs no tracking, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing here.
