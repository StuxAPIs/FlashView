Cookies Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026

Short version

FlashView sets no cookies: none for tracking, advertising or analytics.

What's stored in your browser

  • stuxapis-theme in localStorage: whether you last chose light or dark mode.
  • Saved data from the Flash movies you play (their “local shared objects”, such as game progress), which Ruffle keeps in localStorage.

localStorage isn't a cookie: it isn't sent to any server and never leaves your device.

Third parties

There are no third-party scripts, ads or trackers, so nothing else can set cookies here either.

Clearing it

Clear this site's data in your browser's settings at any time. This also deletes the movies' saves.
