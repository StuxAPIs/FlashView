Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026

The short version

FlashView is a static website with no server-side code, no database, no accounts and no analytics. We don't collect anything about you.

Files you open

When you open an SWF from your device, your browser reads it and plays it on the page. The file isn't uploaded to us or anyone else.

Links you play

When you play an SWF from a link, your browser downloads it straight from that link's site, which sees the request as it would any visit. The link also goes into the page's address (?url=) so you can share it; we don't receive or store it.

What the movies do

A Flash movie can save data (its “local shared objects”, such as game saves), which Ruffle keeps in your browser's local storage on this site. A movie can also try to open links or contact other sites; FlashView asks you before opening a link.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical request details (like IP addresses) for security and operations, under GitHub's privacy statement. Every file, the fonts and Ruffle included, comes from this same domain.

Sites that embed our Ruffle

If another site loads Ruffle from FlashView, your browser fetches the Ruffle files from us. We don't run anything else on those requests and don't see what you play there.

Changes

If FlashView ever collects data, this policy will be updated first to say exactly what and why. The date above marks the latest revision.
