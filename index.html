Play SWF files in your browser

Open an old Flash game or animation from your computer, or from a link, and play it right here. No plugin, no download, and your file never leaves your device.

How it works

Stays on your device

A file you open is read by your own browser and played there. It isn't uploaded anywhere.

Links work too

Paste a link to an .swf and it plays from there. The site hosting it has to allow it (CORS). Share ?url= links to send someone straight to a movie.

Powered by Ruffle

FlashView runs Ruffle, an open-source Flash Player emulator, served from this site. Older ActionScript 1 and 2 content works best; ActionScript 3 support is still growing.

Use it on your site

Add one script tag and Ruffle plays the Flash embeds already on your pages. See Embed.
